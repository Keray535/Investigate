# T-024: True-peak follow-up: per-tier tap count, and EBU cases 20 to 23
- Owner: dsp-engineer
- Milestone: M1
- Status: DONE
- Depends on: T-007

## Goal
Close the two gaps T-007 left open:
- **Performance.** `MeterBank::process` has a p99 of up to 38.8 µs against the 40 µs budget, because `TruePeakDetector`'s shared delay line grew from 12 to 25 taps for the 88.2/96 kHz fix. The 44.1/48 kHz tier now multiplies through zero-padded taps. Give each oversampling tier its own tap count, so no zero coefficients are ever multiplied.
- **Conformance.** EBU Tech 3341 true-peak cases 20 to 23 have no automated test. Add them, with an oracle that is validated separately from the meter under test.

The M1 milestone closes when this task is DONE.

## Inputs
- `source/dsp/TruePeakDetector.h/.cpp`, `source/dsp/MeterBank.h/.cpp`, and the T-007 task log, which covers the design history and the two rejected designs.
- `docs/research/T-007-true-peak-coefficients.md` and `docs/research/T-006-ebu-test-cases.md` (cases 20 to 23).
- `docs/qa/T-007-unit.md`, which includes why the earlier synthesis attempts for cases 20 to 23 were withdrawn.
- The roadmap: Budgets, and the Decisions dated 2026-09-28.

## Design requirements
- **Per-tier kernels.** The 4x tier uses exactly 12 taps per phase (the literal ITU table). The 2x tier keeps its current 49-tap, 2-phase design. The 1x tier is a direct sample peak. The inner loops must not process zero-padding. Separate code paths, templates or per-tier constants are all acceptable. The results must stay bit-identical, or within 1e-6 dB, to the current T-007 outputs on the existing test signals.
- **Synthesis of cases 20 to 23** (a test-side design, written by unit-tester in the coder-tester loop):
  1. Generate the case signal at 4·fs, as `docs/research/T-006-ebu-test-cases.md` describes: an fs/6 sine at 0.50 FFS containing one phase-continuous period of an fs/4 sine at amplitude 1.00, with short fades.
  2. Apply a documented linear-phase anti-alias low-pass (for example a Kaiser-windowed sinc with its cutoff at or below 0.45·fs of the target rate and at least 90 dB stop-band attenuation).
  3. Downsample to fs with offsets 0, 1, 2 and 3.
  4. **Oracle validation.** Before testing the meter, assert that the peak of the anti-alias-filtered 4·fs signal is 0.0 ±0.1 dBFS. If that fails, the synthesis is wrong, and the test reports a synthesis failure, not a meter failure.
  5. **Meter test.** The detector's true-peak max on each downsampled signal is within +0.2/−0.4 dB of 0.0 dBTP, at 48 kHz.
  6. **Second opinion.** libebur128's true peak on the same signals is recorded in the QA report for information.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] In the 4x tier (44.1/48 kHz) the inner loop runs exactly 12 multiply-adds per phase per sample; the 2x tier runs 25 or fewer per phase. Shown by code review, and by a unit test or code comment that asserts the kernel sizes.
- [x] All existing T-004 to T-007 tests pass unchanged. True-peak outputs on the existing T-007 test signals match the previous implementation within 1e-6 dB.
- [x] EBU Tech 3341 cases 20, 21, 22 and 23 each pass: the oracle validation holds, and the meter is within +0.2/−0.4 dB of 0.0 dBTP at 48 kHz. `docs/qa/T-024-unit.md` includes a results table.
- [x] `MeterBank::process`, 128-sample stereo at 48 kHz, Release, run in isolation with the `[perf]` tag: mean ≤ 10 µs and p99 ≤ 30 µs across 3 runs, with the numbers reported.
- [x] Warning-clean at `/W4`. `docs/qa/T-024-unit.md` reports PASS.

Status set to IN_PROGRESS. Design plan before coding:
  - **Root cause of the perf gap**: `TruePeakDetector::ChannelState` has one `delay` array sized `kTapsPerPhase=25` (`ceil(49/2)`, needed only by the 88.2/96 kHz 49-tap/factor-2 Hann-sinc design) and one `computePhase()` loop, shared by both the 4x ITU table and the 2x Hann-sinc table so they could reuse the same delay line/loop. The ITU table's 12 real taps per phase are zero-padded to 25 to fit that shared array. At 44.1/48 kHz (`oversampleFactor==4`, the two most common project sample rates and therefore the ones the isolated perf test exercises) every sample: (a) shifts a 25-element delay line (`pushSample`, O(25) copies) when only 12 are ever read, and (b) runs 4 phases x 25 multiply-adds (100 total) of which 4 x 13 = 52 are `coeff * anything` with `coeff` an exact `0.0f` literal - contributing nothing to the sum (adding `0.0f` cannot change a finite float sum's value or rounding, so this wastes cycles, not correctness) but still costing real multiply-add instructions every sample, every block. This is the direct, measured cause of T-007's thin p99 margin (up to 38.8 us against the 40 us budget, per the roadmap's T-007 decision row).
  - **Fix**: decouple the two tiers' storage instead of sharing one padded array/loop. `ChannelState` gets two independently-sized delay lines: `delay4x` (`std::array<float, kItuTapsPerPhase>`, `kItuTapsPerPhase=12`, exact - no padding) and `delay2x` (`std::array<float, kHannSincTapsPerPhase>`, `kHannSincTapsPerPhase=25`, unchanged from today - the task only asks that the 2x tier "keep its current 49-tap, 2-phase design", not that its own internal near-zero coefficients be trimmed further; 25 is already inside the task's "25 or fewer per phase" bound for that tier). Two small push/compute method pairs (`push4x`/`computePhase4x` over 12 elements, `push2x`/`computePhase2x` over 25 elements) replace the single shared `pushSample`/`computePhase`. `kPhaseCoeffs` (the ITU table) shrinks from `array<array<float,25>,4>` to `array<array<float,12>,4>` with the 13 trailing zero columns deleted outright (not just left at 0 - removing them from the table is what makes "no zero-padded taps are ever multiplied" true by construction, not just by accident of the data). `kHannSincPhaseCoeffs` is untouched (still `array<array<float,25>,2>` - its own phase-0 column being 24 exact zeros plus one 1.0 is a genuine mathematical property of an odd-length/factor-2 polyphase design, per the existing class comment on `sin(m*pi/2)==0`, not a padding artefact this task's "no zero-padded taps" wording is about; the task explicitly scopes that requirement to "the 4x tier" and leaves the 2x tier as "its current design"). `process()`'s branch on `oversampleFactor` picks `push4x`/`computePhase4x` + `kPhaseCoeffs` for `==4`, `push2x`/`computePhase2x` + `kHannSincPhaseCoeffs` for `==2`, and the existing direct-`std::abs(x)` path, untouched, for `==1`. `reset()` clears both `delay4x` and `delay2x` unconditionally (cheap, only 37 floats/channel, and avoids a branch on `oversampleFactor` in a function that is not itself hot).
  - **Why this stays bit-identical, not just within 1e-6 dB, on the existing 4x-tier test signals**: removing multiplications by an exact `0.0f` coefficient from a finite-float sum cannot change the sum's value or its rounding at any intermediate step (`x + 0.0f == x` exactly, for every finite `x`, and `0.0f * y == 0.0f` exactly for every finite `y`) - so `computePhase4x`'s 12-term sum is bit-for-bit the old 25-term sum with 13 provably-zero-valued addends removed, not an approximation of it. The 2x tier's arrays, loop and results are untouched entirely. The acceptance criterion's "within 1e-6 dB" wording is satisfied trivially (0 ULP difference), kept as the acceptance bound only because that is what the task asks a test to assert.
  - **Expected perf effect**: at 44.1/48 kHz, per-sample cost drops from a 25-element shift + 100 multiply-adds (4 phases x 25) to a 12-element shift + 48 multiply-adds (4 phases x 12) - roughly halving `TruePeakDetector`'s per-sample cost at the tier the isolated 48 kHz perf test exercises, which is expected to restore `MeterBank::process()` to comfortably inside mean <=10 us / p99 <=30 us (T-007 measured 8.9-10.6 us mean / 32.9-38.8 us p99 isolated with the old 25-tap-everywhere design; halving the true-peak inner loop's cost at 48 kHz should remove most of the p99 margin problem, since true-peak filtering was T-007's own diagnosed cause of the regression from 5.43 us to 8.9+ us mean). Will measure and report the actual numbers after building, not assume them.
  - **EBU cases 20-23**: this task's own design requirement hands the test synthesis to `unit-tester` with a specific two-stage oracle (validate the 4*fs anti-alias-filtered signal's own peak first, at 0.0 +-0.1 dBFS, before asserting the meter against +0.2/-0.4 dB) - exactly the check T-007's two withdrawn attempts never did, which is why (per `docs/qa/T-007-unit.md`'s open question 1) they could not tell a synthesis bug from a detector bug.
