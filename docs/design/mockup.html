Investigate editor mockup for T-010, with spectrum zoom and pan from T-034 Simulated audio. The controls above this line belong to the preview, not the plugin.
Size or drag the grip at the bottom-right corner
Try: hover the spectrum, click it, right-click it, drag or scroll any chip, double-click to reset, click a value to type.
Zoom and pan: scroll over the spectrum to zoom frequency (Shift: fine) · Ctrl+scroll, or scroll over the dB labels, to zoom level · scroll over the frequency labels · drag (left or middle) to pan · double-click or Fit for the full view · Ctrl+drag to zoom into a box (Esc cancels) · drag or click the overview strip · sideways touchpad swipe pans. A single click resets the max/avg curves after 250 ms. Set Block to 512 and zoom in to see the bin-spacing hint. A touchpad pinch zooms level, as it will in the plugin.
